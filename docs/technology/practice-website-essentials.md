# Practice Website Essentials

## Overview

Your website is often the first impression potential patients have of your Direct Primary Care practice. For many people unfamiliar with DPC, your website must educate, build trust, and convert visitors into members—all while reflecting your practice values.

This guide covers what your website needs to accomplish and how to build it effectively on any budget.

## Prerequisites

- Practice name and branding decisions made (or in progress)
- Pricing structure determined (see [Pricing Your Practice](../pricing-membership/pricing-your-practice.md))
- Understanding of your target patient demographics

---

## Website Goals

Your DPC website must accomplish four primary goals:

### 1. Educate
Most visitors do not know what Direct Primary Care is. Your website must explain:
- What DPC means
- How it differs from traditional care and insurance
- What patients receive

### 2. Build Trust
Healthcare is personal. Visitors need to feel confident in you before joining:
- Physician credentials and background
- Practice philosophy
- Social proof (testimonials, reviews)

### 3. Inform
Practical information for decision-making:
- Services included
- Pricing (transparency is a DPC advantage)
- Location and hours
- How to join

### 4. Convert
Turn visitors into members:
- Clear call-to-action
- Easy enrollment or inquiry process
- Contact options

## Essential Pages

### Homepage

**Purpose:** Capture attention, communicate value, direct visitors to key information.

**Elements to Include:**
- Clear headline explaining what you offer
- Brief DPC explanation (2-3 sentences)
- Your photo and brief introduction
- Key benefits (3-4 points)
- Call-to-action (Schedule visit, Learn more, Join)
- Navigation to other key pages

**Sample Headline Approaches:**
- "Primary Care the Way It Should Be"
- "Unlimited Access to Your Personal Physician"
- "Membership-Based Healthcare Without Insurance Hassles"
- "More Time. Better Care. Direct Access."

---

### About / Meet the Doctor

**Purpose:** Build personal connection and trust.

**Elements to Include:**
- Professional photo (friendly, approachable)
- Medical credentials and training
- Professional experience
- Why you chose DPC
- Personal touches (family, hobbies, community involvement)
- Practice philosophy

**Tips:**
- Be authentic, not overly formal
- Show personality while maintaining professionalism
- Explain your "why"—patients connect with purpose
- Include any staff if applicable

---

### What is Direct Primary Care?

**Purpose:** Educate visitors unfamiliar with the DPC model.

**Elements to Include:**

**What DPC Is:**
- Membership-based primary care
- Monthly fee covers most/all primary care services
- No insurance billing, no copays, no surprise bills
- Direct relationship with physician

**What DPC Is NOT:**
- Not health insurance
- Not concierge medicine (explain difference if relevant)
- Does not replace need for insurance (catastrophic/hospital coverage)

**Benefits:**
- More time with your doctor
- Same-day or next-day appointments
- Direct communication (call, text, email)
- Transparent pricing
- Focus on prevention and wellness

**Comparison Table (Optional but Effective):**

| Feature | Traditional Primary Care | Direct Primary Care |
|---------|-------------------------|---------------------|
| Appointment access | Days to weeks | Same day or next day |
| Visit length | 10-15 minutes | 30-60 minutes |
| Physician communication | Through staff, portals | Direct access |
| Pricing | Copays, deductibles, surprise bills | Transparent monthly fee |
| Physician panel size | 2,000-3,000 patients | 400-800 patients |

---

### Services

**Purpose:** Detail what membership includes (and doesn't include).

**Structure:**
- List of included services
- Any services with additional cost (labs, procedures, etc.)
- What is NOT included (and why insurance is still recommended)

**Included Services (Example):**
- Unlimited office visits
- Annual comprehensive physical exam
- Same-day sick visits
- Chronic disease management (diabetes, hypertension, etc.)
- Preventive care and screenings
- Telehealth/video visits
- Direct physician communication
- Care coordination
- Prescription management
- Basic in-office procedures
- EKG, spirometry (list what applies)

**Additional Cost Services:**
- Laboratory tests (at wholesale pricing)
- Vaccines (at cost)
- Certain procedures and supplies
- Referral visits to specialists

---

### Pricing / Membership

**Purpose:** Transparent pricing (a key DPC differentiator).

**Elements to Include:**
- Membership tiers/categories with prices
- What is included at each level
- Family/household pricing
- Enrollment fee (if any)
- Payment methods accepted
- Annual payment discount (if offered)

**Example Pricing Display:**

| Membership | Monthly Fee |
|------------|-------------|
| Children (0-17) | $50/month |
| Adults (18-64) | $89/month |
| Seniors (65+) | $109/month |
| Family Cap | $250/month |

*Annual payment: 10% discount*

**Important Disclosures:**
- This is not insurance
- Recommend maintaining health insurance for hospital/emergency/specialist coverage
- Link to FAQ or What is DPC page for details

---

### Contact / Location

**Purpose:** Make it easy to reach you and find you.

**Elements to Include:**
- Physical address (with map)
- Phone number
- Email address
- Office hours
- Contact form
- Directions/parking notes

**Important:** Make phone/email clickable on mobile.

---

### Enrollment / Join

**Purpose:** Convert interested visitors into members.

**Options:**
- Contact form to request information/consultation
- Online enrollment form
- Scheduling tool for meet-and-greet appointment
- Link to patient portal enrollment

**Recommended Approach:**
Offer low-commitment first step: "Schedule a Free Meet-and-Greet" or "Request Information" rather than requiring immediate commitment.

**Information to Potentially Collect:**
- Name and contact information
- Insurance status (for educational purposes)
- How they heard about you
- Any specific health concerns
- Preferred contact method

---

### FAQ

**Purpose:** Address common questions and objections.

**Common Questions to Address:**
- What if I need to see a specialist?
- What about emergencies?
- Do I still need health insurance?
- What if I move or need to cancel?
- How is this different from concierge medicine?
- Do you accept Medicare/Medicaid? (Be clear on your policy)
- What about prescriptions?
- Can my whole family join?
- How do I schedule appointments?

---

### Blog / Resources (Optional but Valuable)

**Purpose:** Provide value, improve SEO, establish expertise.

**Content Ideas:**
- Health education articles
- DPC explanations and benefits
- Practice updates
- Community health resources
- Prevention and wellness tips

**Note:** Only commit to a blog if you will update it regularly. An abandoned blog looks worse than no blog.

## Technical Considerations

### Platform Options

**Website Builders (Easiest):**

| Platform | Cost | Best For |
|----------|------|----------|
| Squarespace | $12-$40/month | Beautiful templates, easy to use |
| Wix | $14-$39/month | Flexible, many features |
| Weebly | $0-$26/month | Simple, affordable |
| WordPress.com | $4-$45/month | Flexible, SEO-friendly |

**For More Control:**

| Platform | Cost | Best For |
|----------|------|----------|
| WordPress.org (self-hosted) | $5-$30/month hosting | Full customization, plugins |
| Webflow | $14-$39/month | Designer quality, no coding |

**Recommendation for Most DPC Practices:**
Squarespace or Wix offer the best balance of quality, ease, and cost for solo practices.

---

### Domain Name

**Tips:**
- Keep it short and memorable
- Include your name or practice name
- Avoid hyphens and numbers
- .com is preferred; .health, .md, or .care are alternatives

**Examples:**
- drmarysmithmd.com
- smithfamilydpc.com
- clearwaterdirectcare.com

**Cost:** $10-$20/year for standard domains

**Registration:** Google Domains, Namecheap, GoDaddy, or through your website builder

---

### Essential Technical Features

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---------|----------------|
| **Mobile responsive** | Majority of visitors use phones |
| **SSL certificate (HTTPS)** | Security; builds trust; required for SEO |
| **Fast loading** | Slow sites lose visitors |
| **Clear navigation** | Visitors find information easily |
| **Contact form** | Easy way to reach you |
| **Analytics** | Track visitor behavior |

---

### HIPAA Considerations

**Your website itself typically does not need to be HIPAA compliant** unless you are collecting Protected Health Information (PHI).

**To stay safe:**
- Don't collect health information via standard contact forms
- If offering patient portal access, use compliant platform
- If accepting online enrollment with health history, use HIPAA-compliant form tools
- Include privacy policy

**HIPAA-Compliant Form Options:**
- JotForm (HIPAA version)
- Formstack (HIPAA version)
- Patient portal systems
- IntakeQ

## Content Best Practices

### Writing for Your Audience

**Do:**
- Use plain language (avoid medical jargon)
- Address patient concerns and questions
- Be warm and approachable
- Focus on benefits, not just features
- Use "you" language (patient-centered)

**Don't:**
- Use overly clinical language
- Make it all about yourself
- Hide pricing or important information
- Use stock photos exclusively (real photos build trust)
- Overwhelm with too much text

---

### Photography

**Recommended Photos:**
- Professional headshot (approachable, not stiff)
- Office/exam room photos
- Candid (with consent) or staged patient interaction photos
- Team photos if applicable
- Community/personal photos (optional)

**Tips:**
- Natural lighting preferred
- Smile genuinely
- Dress professionally but approachably
- Invest in professional photography if budget allows ($200-$500)

**Stock Photo Caution:**
Visitors can tell stock photos. Use sparingly, if at all, for people photos. Stock photos of objects/backgrounds are more acceptable.

---

### Testimonials and Social Proof

**Options:**
- Patient testimonials (with written permission)
- Google/Yelp review excerpts
- Physician credentials and affiliations
- Media mentions
- Community involvement

**Testimonial Best Practices:**
- Get written permission
- Use first name and last initial
- Specific testimonials are more powerful than generic
- Video testimonials are highly effective if available

## SEO Basics

### What to Know

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) helps patients find you through Google.

**Essential Steps:**
- Include location in key places (title, headings, content)
- Use relevant keywords naturally ("direct primary care [city]")
- Complete Google Business Profile listing
- Ensure mobile-friendly design
- Get loading speed optimized
- Build local citations (directories)

**Target Keywords:**
- Direct primary care [city name]
- DPC physician [city name]
- Family doctor [city name]
- Primary care membership [city name]
- Concierge medicine [city name] (people may search this even though different)

---

### Google Business Profile

**Critical for local search visibility.**

**Setup:**
1. Claim or create listing at business.google.com
2. Verify ownership (usually via postcard)
3. Complete all information
4. Add photos
5. Encourage patient reviews
6. Post updates occasionally

**Include:**
- Practice name
- Address
- Phone
- Website
- Hours
- Categories (Physician, Family Practice, Direct Primary Care if available)
- Services
- Photos

## Budget Approaches

### Minimal Budget ($100-$300)

**DIY Approach:**
- Wix or Squarespace free trial, then basic plan (~$14-$18/month)
- Do your own photos (smartphone in good light)
- Write your own content
- Free logo maker (Canva) or simple text-based logo

**Timeline:** Can launch in 1-2 weeks

---

### Moderate Budget ($500-$1,500)

**Semi-DIY:**
- Website builder premium plan
- Professional headshot photography (~$150-$300)
- Simple logo from Fiverr or 99designs (~$50-$300)
- Template customization
- Professional domain

**Timeline:** 2-4 weeks

---

### Higher Budget ($2,000-$5,000+)

**Professional Help:**
- Web designer/developer
- Custom design
- Professional photography throughout
- Custom branding/logo
- Copywriting services
- SEO optimization

**Timeline:** 4-8 weeks

**Note:** This level is rarely necessary for a solo DPC practice. Simple and professional often outperforms elaborate and complex.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Hiding Pricing
**Problem:** Visitors leave to find practices with transparent pricing.
**Solution:** DPC's transparency is an advantage—display pricing prominently.

### Mistake 2: Too Much Text
**Problem:** Visitors skim; walls of text are ignored.
**Solution:** Use headings, bullets, and short paragraphs.

### Mistake 3: No Clear Call-to-Action
**Problem:** Visitors don't know what to do next.
**Solution:** Every page should have a clear next step.

### Mistake 4: Outdated Information
**Problem:** Destroys trust; signals neglect.
**Solution:** Keep hours, pricing, and news current.

### Mistake 5: Neglecting Mobile
**Problem:** Most visitors use phones; poor mobile experience loses patients.
**Solution:** Test on multiple devices; prioritize mobile design.
