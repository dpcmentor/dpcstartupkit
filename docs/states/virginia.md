# Virginia DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Virginia has DPC-enabling legislation (Va. Code § 38.2-4601) and permits physician medication dispensing with registration. Proximity to DC creates opportunities in Northern Virginia and throughout the state.

---

!!! warning "Caution"
    **Verify Current Law:** Always confirm current regulations with the [Virginia Board of Medicine](https://www.dhp.virginia.gov/medicine/) and review [Code of Virginia Title 38.2](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title38.2/) for the most current statutory language.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Statute:** Code of Virginia § 38.2-4601

**Statute URL:** [Code of Virginia Title 38.2](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title38.2/)

Virginia has DPC-enabling legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

**Key provisions:**
- DPC agreements not considered insurance
- Primary care services defined
- Written agreement required

### What This Means

- DPC practices operate outside insurance regulatory framework
- Legal clarity for membership model
- Standard business regulations apply

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Virginia permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with Virginia Board of Pharmacy
- **Pharmacy Board:** [dhp.virginia.gov/pharmacy](https://www.dhp.virginia.gov/pharmacy/)
- **PMP Requirement:** Check Virginia Prescription Monitoring Program
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

## State-Specific Considerations

### Northern Virginia

- DC suburb, high income
- Federal employees (FEHB considerations)
- Competitive market
- Higher cost of living

### Richmond/Hampton Roads

- Established medical communities
- Growing DPC presence
- More moderate costs

### Shenandoah Valley and Rural

- Primary care shortage
- Lower overhead
- Telehealth valuable

### Federal Employees

- Large federal workforce
- Consider FEHB implications
- May need to explain DPC relationship to federal benefits

---

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Virginia

- [ ] Obtain or verify Virginia medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with State Corporation Commission
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register with Board of Pharmacy (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
