# Oregon DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Oregon has DPC-enabling legislation (O.R.S. § 735.500) and permits physician medication dispensing with registration. Active DPC community in Portland metro with rural opportunities statewide.

---

!!! warning "Caution"
    **Verify Current Law:** Always confirm current regulations with the [Oregon Medical Board](https://www.oregon.gov/omb/) and review [Oregon Revised Statutes Chapter 735](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors735.html) for the most current statutory language.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Statute:** Oregon Revised Statutes § 735.500

**Statute URL:** [Oregon Revised Statutes Chapter 735](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors735.html)

Oregon has DPC-enabling legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

**Key provisions:**
- DPC agreements not considered insurance
- Primary care services defined
- Written agreement required

### What This Means

- DPC practices operate outside insurance regulatory framework
- Legal clarity for membership model
- Standard business regulations apply

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Oregon permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with Oregon Board of Pharmacy
- **Pharmacy Board:** [oregon.gov/pharmacy](https://www.oregon.gov/pharmacy/)
- **PDMP Requirement:** Check Oregon PDMP for controlled substances
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

## State-Specific Considerations

### Portland Metro

- Active DPC presence
- Health-conscious population
- Differentiation important

### No Sales Tax

- Oregon has no sales tax
- Simplifies pricing/membership fees

### Rural Oregon

- Significant primary care shortage
- Eastern Oregon underserved
- Telehealth essential for coverage

### Higher Cost of Living

- Portland metro has higher costs
- Factor into pricing decisions
- Rural areas more affordable

---

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Oregon

- [ ] Obtain or verify Oregon medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with Secretary of State
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register with Board of Pharmacy (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
