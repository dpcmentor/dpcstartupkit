# Illinois DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Illinois does not have explicit DPC-enabling legislation. Medication dispensing has restrictions. Large market in Chicago metro but legal consultation recommended.

---

> [!CAUTION]
> **Verify Current Law:** Illinois lacks explicit DPC legislation. Consult the [Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation](https://idfpr.illinois.gov/) and a healthcare attorney to ensure compliance.

---

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Status:** No explicit DPC-enabling legislation

Illinois has not enacted specific legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

### What This Means

- Legal status of DPC less certain
- Consult healthcare attorney before starting
- Structure agreements carefully
- Monitor for legislative developments

---

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Illinois has **restrictions** on physician dispensing.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Restrictions:** Dispensing in specific circumstances
- **Pharmacy Board:** [idfpr.illinois.gov](https://idfpr.illinois.gov/profs/Pharmacist.asp)
- **Review Regulations:** Consult Board before implementing dispensing

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Illinois

- [ ] **Consult healthcare attorney** regarding DPC legal structure
- [ ] Obtain or verify Illinois medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with Secretary of State
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Review dispensing restrictions carefully
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement (attorney review essential)
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
