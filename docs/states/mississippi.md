# Mississippi DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Mississippi has DPC-enabling legislation (Miss. Code Ann. § 83-9-353) and permits physician medication dispensing. Low cost of living and significant rural opportunities for DPC practice.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Statute:** Mississippi Code Annotated § 83-9-353

**Statute URL:** [Mississippi Code Title 83](https://law.justia.com/codes/mississippi/title-83/)

Mississippi has DPC-enabling legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

**Key provisions:**
- DPC agreements not considered insurance
- Primary care services defined
- Written agreement required

### What This Means

- DPC practices operate outside insurance regulatory framework
- Legal clarity for membership model
- Standard business regulations apply

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Mississippi permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with Mississippi Board of Pharmacy
- **Pharmacy Board:** [mbp.ms.gov](https://www.mbp.ms.gov/)
- **Controlled Substances:** Permitted with proper registration
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

## State-Specific Considerations

### Lowest Cost of Living

- Among lowest cost-of-living states
- Very low overhead possible
- Competitive membership pricing feasible

### Rural Primary Care Crisis

- Significant primary care shortage statewide
- DPC can address major access gaps
- Telehealth essential for coverage

### Jackson Metro

- Limited DPC presence currently
- Opportunity for early movers

---

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Mississippi

- [ ] Obtain or verify Mississippi medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with Secretary of State
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register with Board of Pharmacy (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
