# Pennsylvania DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Pennsylvania does not have explicit DPC-enabling legislation. Medication dispensing is permitted with registration. Large population with opportunities in Philadelphia and Pittsburgh metros. Legal consultation recommended.

---

!!! warning "Caution"
    **Verify Current Law:** Pennsylvania lacks explicit DPC legislation. Consult the [Pennsylvania State Board of Medicine](https://www.dos.pa.gov/ProfessionalLicensing/BoardsCommissions/Medicine/Pages/default.aspx) and a healthcare attorney to ensure compliance.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Status:** No explicit DPC-enabling legislation

Pennsylvania has not enacted specific legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

### What This Means

- Legal status of DPC less certain
- Consult healthcare attorney before starting
- Structure agreements carefully
- Monitor for legislative developments

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Pennsylvania permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with Pennsylvania State Board of Pharmacy
- **Pharmacy Board:** [dos.pa.gov/ProfessionalLicensing/BoardsCommissions/Pharmacy](https://www.dos.pa.gov/ProfessionalLicensing/BoardsCommissions/Pharmacy/Pages/default.aspx)
- **PDMP:** Check Pennsylvania Prescription Drug Monitoring Program
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

## State-Specific Considerations

### Two Major Markets

- **Philadelphia:** East coast, higher cost of living
- **Pittsburgh:** Midwest character, more affordable

### Central Pennsylvania

- Rural character
- Primary care needs
- State College, Harrisburg areas

### Large Population

- Fifth most populous state
- Significant market opportunity

### Aging Population

- Pennsylvania has older demographics
- Medicare considerations important

---

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Pennsylvania

- [ ] **Consult healthcare attorney** regarding DPC legal structure
- [ ] Obtain or verify Pennsylvania medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with Department of State
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register with Board of Pharmacy (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement (attorney review essential)
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
