# South Carolina DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** South Carolina has DPC-enabling legislation (S.C. Code Ann. § 38-71-2010) and permits medication dispensing with registration. Growing state with opportunities in Charleston, Columbia, and Greenville metros.

---

!!! warning "Caution"
    **Verify Current Law:** Always confirm current regulations with the [South Carolina Board of Medical Examiners](https://llr.sc.gov/med/) and review [South Carolina Code Title 38](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/title38.php) for the most current statutory language.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Statute:** South Carolina Code Annotated § 38-71-2010

**Statute URL:** [South Carolina Code Title 38](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/title38.php)

South Carolina has DPC-enabling legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

**Key provisions:**
- DPC agreements not considered insurance
- Primary care services defined
- Written agreement required

### What This Means

- DPC practices have legal clarity
- Standard business regulations apply
- Supportive regulatory environment

---

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

South Carolina permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with South Carolina Board of Pharmacy
- **Pharmacy Board:** [llr.sc.gov/bop](https://llr.sc.gov/bop/)
- **PDMP:** Check South Carolina Prescription Monitoring Program
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

## State-Specific Considerations

### Growing State

- One of faster-growing states
- Retirement destination
- Increasing healthcare demand

### Three Major Markets

- **Charleston:** Coastal, growing rapidly
- **Columbia:** State capital, central location
- **Greenville:** Upstate, manufacturing base

### Coastal Considerations

- Hurricane planning important
- Seasonal population in beach areas

### Retirement Population

- Significant retiree population
- Medicare considerations important

---

## Checklist for Starting DPC in South Carolina

- [ ] Obtain or verify South Carolina medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with Secretary of State
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register with Board of Pharmacy (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
