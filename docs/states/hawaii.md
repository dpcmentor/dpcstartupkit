# Hawaii DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Hawaii does not have explicit DPC-enabling legislation. Medication dispensing is permitted with registration. Unique island geography and high cost of living create distinct practice considerations.

---

!!! warning "Caution"
    **Verify Current Law:** Hawaii lacks explicit DPC legislation. Consult the [Hawaii Medical Board](https://cca.hawaii.gov/pvl/boards/medical/) and a healthcare attorney to ensure compliance.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Status:** No explicit DPC-enabling legislation

Hawaii has not enacted specific legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

### What This Means

- Legal status of DPC less certain
- Consult healthcare attorney before starting
- Structure agreements carefully
- Monitor for legislative developments

---

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Hawaii permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with Hawaii Board of Pharmacy
- **Pharmacy Board:** [cca.hawaii.gov/pvl/boards/pharmacy](https://cca.hawaii.gov/pvl/boards/pharmacy/)
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

## State-Specific Considerations

### Highest Cost of Living

- Highest or near-highest cost of living nationally
- Higher membership fees necessary
- Supply chain adds costs

### Island Geography

- Separate markets on each island
- Oahu has most population
- Inter-island logistics complex

### Limited Specialist Access

- Fewer specialists available
- Strong referral network critical
- Mainland telehealth partnerships valuable

### Tourism Economy

- Consider seasonal fluctuations
- Military population significant

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Hawaii

- [ ] **Consult healthcare attorney** regarding DPC legal structure
- [ ] Obtain or verify Hawaii medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with DCCA
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register with Board of Pharmacy (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement (attorney review essential)
- [ ] Plan medication supply chain logistics
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
