# North Dakota DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** North Dakota has DPC-enabling legislation (N.D.C.C. § 26.1-04-24) and permits medication dispensing. Small population but significant rural primary care needs. Business-friendly environment.

---

!!! warning "Caution"
    **Verify Current Law:** Always confirm current regulations with the [North Dakota Board of Medicine](https://www.ndbom.org/) and review [North Dakota Century Code Title 26.1](https://www.ndlegis.gov/cencode/t26-1.html) for the most current statutory language.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Statute:** North Dakota Century Code § 26.1-04-24

**Statute URL:** [North Dakota Century Code Title 26.1](https://www.ndlegis.gov/cencode/t26-1.html)

North Dakota has DPC-enabling legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

**Key provisions:**
- DPC agreements not considered insurance
- Primary care services defined
- Written agreement required

### What This Means

- DPC practices have legal clarity
- Standard business regulations apply
- Supportive regulatory environment

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

North Dakota permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with North Dakota Board of Pharmacy
- **Pharmacy Board:** [nodakpharmacy.com](https://www.nodakpharmacy.com/)
- **PDMP:** Check North Dakota Prescription Drug Monitoring Program
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

## State-Specific Considerations

### Small Population

- Among least populous states
- Limited patient pool
- Fargo and Bismarck have most opportunity

### Rural Character

- Vast rural areas
- Significant primary care shortage
- Telehealth essential

### No State Income Tax

- North Dakota has relatively low state income tax
- Business-friendly environment

### Oil Industry

- Western ND oil industry
- Employer-sponsored opportunities
- Transient workforce considerations

---

## Checklist for Starting DPC in North Dakota

- [ ] Obtain or verify North Dakota medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with Secretary of State
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register with Board of Pharmacy (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement
- [ ] Establish robust telehealth capabilities
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
