# Delaware DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Delaware does not have explicit DPC-enabling legislation. Medication dispensing is permitted with registration. Small state with limited but focused market.

---

!!! warning "Caution"
    **Verify Current Law:** Delaware lacks explicit DPC legislation. Consult the [Delaware Board of Medical Licensure](https://dpr.delaware.gov/boards/medicalpractice/) and a healthcare attorney to ensure compliance.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Status:** No explicit DPC-enabling legislation

Delaware has not enacted specific legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

### What This Means

- Legal status of DPC less certain
- Consult healthcare attorney before starting
- Structure agreements carefully
- Monitor for legislative developments

---

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Delaware permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with Delaware Board of Pharmacy
- **Pharmacy Board:** [dpr.delaware.gov/boards/pharmacy](https://dpr.delaware.gov/boards/pharmacy/)
- **PDMP:** Check Delaware Prescription Monitoring Program
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

## State-Specific Considerations

### Small State

- Second smallest state
- Limited but focused market
- Wilmington area has most opportunity

### Business-Friendly

- Delaware known for corporate law
- Easy entity formation
- No sales tax

### Proximity to Philadelphia

- Many residents work in Philadelphia
- Consider commuter population
- Cross-state considerations

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Delaware

- [ ] **Consult healthcare attorney** regarding DPC legal structure
- [ ] Obtain or verify Delaware medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with Division of Corporations
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register with Board of Pharmacy (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement (attorney review essential)
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
