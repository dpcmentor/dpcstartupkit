# Wisconsin DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Wisconsin has DPC-enabling legislation (Wis. Stat. § 609.035) and permits medication dispensing with registration. Strong healthcare market with opportunities in Milwaukee, Madison, and rural areas.

---

!!! warning "Caution"
    **Verify Current Law:** Always confirm current regulations with the [Wisconsin Medical Examining Board](https://dsps.wi.gov/pages/BoardsCouncils/MEB/Default.aspx) and review [Wisconsin Statutes Chapter 609](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/statutes/statutes/609) for the most current statutory language.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Statute:** Wisconsin Statutes § 609.035

**Statute URL:** [Wisconsin Statutes Chapter 609](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/statutes/statutes/609)

Wisconsin has DPC-enabling legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

**Key provisions:**
- DPC agreements not considered insurance
- Primary care services defined
- Written agreement required

### What This Means

- DPC practices have legal clarity
- Standard business regulations apply
- Supportive regulatory environment

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Wisconsin permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with Wisconsin Pharmacy Examining Board
- **Pharmacy Board:** [dsps.wi.gov/pages/BoardsCouncils/Pharmacy/Default.aspx](https://dsps.wi.gov/pages/BoardsCouncils/Pharmacy/Default.aspx)
- **PDMP:** Check Wisconsin ePDMP
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

## State-Specific Considerations

### Strong Healthcare Market

- Multiple health systems
- Academic medical centers (UW, Medical College of Wisconsin)
- Good specialist access

### Two Major Markets

- **Milwaukee:** Largest city, established healthcare
- **Madison:** State capital, university town

### Rural Wisconsin

- Northern Wisconsin rural
- Primary care shortage
- Telehealth valuable

### Manufacturing Base

- Strong manufacturing employers
- Employer-sponsored DPC opportunities

---

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Wisconsin

- [ ] Obtain or verify Wisconsin medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with DFI
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register with Pharmacy Examining Board (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
