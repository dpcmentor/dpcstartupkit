# Washington DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Washington has DPC-enabling legislation (RCW 48.150) and permits physician medication dispensing with registration. Active DPC community in Seattle metro with rural opportunities statewide.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Statute:** Revised Code of Washington Chapter 48.150

**Statute URL:** [RCW 48.150](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=48.150)

Washington was among the first states with DPC-enabling legislation.

**Key provisions:**
- DPC agreements not considered insurance
- Primary care services defined
- Written agreement required
- Consumer disclosure requirements

### What This Means

- DPC practices operate outside insurance regulatory framework
- Legal clarity for membership model
- Standard business regulations apply

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Washington permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with Washington State Department of Health
- **Pharmacy Commission:** [doh.wa.gov/licenses-permits-and-certificates/professions-new-renew-702/pharmacies](https://doh.wa.gov/licenses-permits-and-certificates/professions-new-renew-702/pharmacies)
- **PMP Requirement:** Check Washington Prescription Monitoring Program
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

## State-Specific Considerations

### Seattle Metro

- Active DPC community
- Tech-savvy population
- Higher cost of living
- Competitive market

### No State Income Tax

- Washington has no state personal income tax
- Improves take-home income

### Eastern Washington

- Primary care shortage
- Lower cost of living
- Rural opportunities
- Telehealth valuable

### Tech Industry

- Large tech employers
- Employer-sponsored DPC opportunities
- Health-conscious workforce

---

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Washington

- [ ] Obtain or verify Washington medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with Secretary of State
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register for dispensing with Department of Health (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
