# Arkansas DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Arkansas has DPC-enabling legislation (A.C.A. § 23-60-107) and permits physician medication dispensing. A low cost-of-living state with rural opportunities for DPC practice.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Statute:** Arkansas Code Annotated § 23-60-107

**Statute URL:** [Arkansas Code Title 23](https://advance.lexis.com/container?config=00JAA3ZTU0NTIzYy0zZDEyLTRhYmQtYmRmMS1iMWIxNDgxYWMxZTQKAFBvZFNob3J0Y3V0TWVudV9iaHRhYmJNdXJy&crid=81c84a05-ca8e-459e-888f-9b204a2351b5)

Arkansas has DPC-enabling legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

**Key provisions:**
- DPC agreements not considered insurance
- Primary care services defined
- Written agreement required

### What This Means

- DPC practices operate outside insurance regulatory framework
- Legal clarity for membership model
- Standard business regulations apply

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Arkansas permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with Arkansas State Board of Pharmacy
- **Pharmacy Board:** [pharmacyboard.arkansas.gov](https://pharmacyboard.arkansas.gov/)
- **Controlled Substances:** Permitted with proper registration
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records per Board requirements

## State-Specific Considerations

### Low Cost of Living

- Among lowest cost-of-living states
- Lower overhead possible
- Competitive membership pricing feasible

### Rural Opportunities

- Significant rural primary care shortage
- DPC can address access gaps
- Telehealth valuable for coverage

---

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Arkansas

- [ ] Obtain or verify Arkansas medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with Secretary of State
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register with Board of Pharmacy (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
