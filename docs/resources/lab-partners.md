# Lab Partners for DPC Practices

> **Quick Summary:** Direct-pay lab pricing can save your patients a large share of what hospital-based labs charge (savings of 50-90% are commonly cited). Building relationships with labs is essential for DPC practices.

!!! note "About the figures on this page"
    Dollar amounts are estimates unless a source is linked next to them. Prices vary by state, vendor and year; treat them as orders of magnitude and confirm before you spend.

---

## Two Rules Before You Sign Anything

**Labs do not need a business associate agreement.** A laboratory that runs the tests you order is a HIPAA covered entity in its own right, and your order is a treatment disclosure, so no BAA is required between you and the lab. Do not let a lab's sales team (or your own caution) hold up account setup over one. See [BAA Requirements](../compliance-legal/baa-requirements.md) for the citation and for the vendors that *do* need one.

**Pass-through lab pricing must be offered to members and non-members alike.** Under the 2026 HSA rules ([IRS Notice 2026-05](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-26-05.pdf), Q&A-11 and Q&A-12), items you bill separately but make available only to members are treated as part of the DPC arrangement. If your lab prices are members-only, a member with a high-deductible health plan can lose HSA eligibility. Publish one lab price list and honour it for anyone who walks in with an order. The HSA note in [Pricing Your Practice](../pricing-membership/pricing-your-practice.md) covers the fee caps and the bundling limits that go with this.

## Lab Partnership Options

The two national reference labs are listed first because most practices start with one of them, not because the kit recommends either. A regional lab may be cheaper, faster or more responsive in your market; compare before you sign.

### National Reference Labs

#### Quest Diagnostics

**DPC-friendly:** Yes

**Account setup:**
- Direct physician account available
- No minimum volume requirements
- Online ordering portal

**Pricing:**
- Negotiated cash pricing for DPC
- Often 50-80% less than hospital labs
- Volume discounts possible

**Patient draw options:**
- 2,000+ patient service centers
- Some offer in-office phlebotomy support
- Mobile phlebotomy in some markets

**Contact:** Quest Diagnostics client services ([questdiagnostics.com](https://www.questdiagnostics.com/))

#### Labcorp

**DPC-friendly:** Yes

**Account setup:**
- Physician office accounts
- Patient pay accounts
- Simple enrollment process

**Pricing:**
- Competitive cash rates
- Published patient pricing available
- Negotiation possible for volume

**Patient draw options:**
- 2,000+ locations nationwide
- At-home collection in some areas

**Contact:** Labcorp client services ([labcorp.com](https://www.labcorp.com/))

### Regional Lab Options

Check your area for regional labs that may offer:
- Better local pricing
- Faster turnaround
- More personalized service
- In-office pickup

**Examples of regional labs (names checked 2026-09-22):**

|------|---------------|--------------|

| HbA1c | DCA Vantage or similar | $8-15/test |
| Lipid panel | CardioChek | $5-10/test |

## Setting Up Lab Accounts

### Quest/Labcorp Process

1. **Contact physician services**
2. **Complete application** (practice info, NPI, license)
3. **Choose billing model:**
   - Bill patient directly (they pay at the patient service center)
   - Bill practice (client billing; you collect from the patient, with or without a markup)
   - Patient pay with negotiated rate
4. **Skip the BAA request** if one is offered; it is not required for a lab (see above)
5. **EMR integration** (if desired)
6. **Receive supplies** (if doing in-office draws)

### Negotiation Tips

- **Mention DPC specifically** - Labs want this volume
- **Ask for DPC pricing** - Different from standard cash pay
- **Discuss volume projections** - Even small panels matter
- **Request price list** - Get it in writing
- **Ask about new tests** - Get additions at same rates
- **Annual review** - Renegotiate periodically

## Lab Workflow Models

### Model 1: Patient Pays Lab Directly

**How it works:**
- You order test
- Patient goes to PSC
- Patient pays lab's cash price

**Pros:**
- Simplest for you
- No accounts receivable
- No markup hassle

**Cons:**
- Less control over pricing
- Patient may be surprised by cost
- Can't build in convenience

### Model 2: You Bill Patient, Pay Lab

**How it works:**
- You order test
- Patient pays you (at time of order or adds to membership)
- Lab bills you
- You pay lab

**Pros:**
- Control pricing
- Can markup modestly
- Bundled patient experience

**Cons:**
- Accounts receivable
- Cash flow management
- More bookkeeping

### Model 3: In-Office Draws, Send Out

**How it works:**
- Draw in your office
- Courier picks up
- Lab runs and bills you

**Pros:**
- Convenient for patients
- All-in-one visit
- Can do some POCT in-house

**Cons:**
- Phlebotomy supplies/training
- Courier coordination
- Specimen handling

## Presenting Lab Costs to Patients

### Transparency Approach

Provide price list of common tests:
- "Here's what we charge for labs"
- "Compare to what you'd pay elsewhere"
- Emphasize savings

### Bundled Approach

Include common labs in membership:
- Annual wellness labs included
- Acute labs at cost
- Premium tier with more included

!!! warning "Subject to change"
    Bundling has an HSA cost. Since 2026 a DPC arrangement that includes laboratory services not typically provided in an ambulatory primary care setting is not a qualifying arrangement at all, and a premium tier that pushes the fee above $150/month per individual (or $300/month for an arrangement covering more than one person) costs those members their HSA eligibility ([IRS Notice 2026-05](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-26-05.pdf)). The [pricing page](../pricing-membership/pricing-your-practice.md) explains why its worked example passes labs through at cost instead. Confirm any bundle with a tax advisor.

### At-Cost Approach

Pass through at your cost:
- "We don't mark up labs"
- Builds trust
- May lose some revenue
- The cleanest fit with the 2026 HSA rules, provided the same price list is available to non-members

**Most DPC EMRs advertise connections to the major labs.** Examples (not a ranking; confirm the current list with each vendor before you sign):

| EMR | Quest | Labcorp | Others |
|-----|-------|---------|--------|

- Electronic ordering
- Results in chart automatically
- Fewer phone calls
- Better tracking

## Common Lab Challenges

### "Why Can't I Use My Insurance?"

**Explain:**
- We get you better cash prices
- Until you meet your deductible, the insurance-billed price is often higher than our cash price
- No surprise bills
- You can still pay with HSA or FSA funds; labs are a qualified medical expense
- If you would rather use insurance, we can send the order to the lab that way and you pay the lab directly

### Patient No-Shows for Labs

**Solutions:**
- In-office draws
- Strong follow-up
- Include in annual visit
- Explain importance

### Getting Results to Patients

**Options:**
- EMR patient portal
- Secure messaging
- Phone call for abnormals
- Scheduled follow-up visit
