# Recommended Reading

## Overview

Learning from those who have built DPC practices—and from broader business and healthcare wisdom—accelerates your success. This list covers DPC-specific resources, practice management, and relevant business fundamentals.

!!! warning "Educational Content Only"
    **This is educational content, not legal or financial advice.**

    - Regulations vary by state and change over time
    - Always consult a healthcare attorney for legal matters
    - Always consult an accountant for tax and financial matters
    - Verify current requirements with official sources

!!! note "How this list was checked"
    Titles, authors and publication years were checked on 2026-09-23. Links go to each publisher's own site; there are no affiliate links and no prices. The kit endorses no book, organization or vendor. Where a site is published by a company that sells to DPC practices, that is stated so you know who is speaking.

## DPC-Specific Resources

### Books

**"Direct Primary Care: The Cure for Our Broken Healthcare System"** by Paul Thomas, MD (self-published, 2018)
- Overview of the DPC model by a Detroit family physician who opened Plum Health DPC in 2016
- Examples drawn from one practice
- Vision for DPC's role in healthcare reform
- The author also sells a companion guide, *Startup DPC: How to Start and Grow Your Direct Primary Care Practice*, through his consulting site [startupdpc.com](https://www.startupdpc.com/books); that site is also the author's coaching business

**Other DPC startup guides:**
- Several physicians and consultants self-publish DPC startup guides
- Check the publication date; the DPC landscape, and especially the HSA rules, changed in 2025–2026
- Note who wrote it: a guide from a consultant, EMR vendor or membership-billing vendor is also that company's marketing

### Articles and Reports

**DPC Frontier Blog**
- Articles on DPC legal and regulatory topics
- Practice insights
- Published by DPC Frontier, LLC, founded in 2015 by Phil Eskew, DO, JD, a DPC physician-attorney; the site carries an attorney-advertising disclaimer
- [dpcfrontier.com/blog](https://www.dpcfrontier.com/blog)

**DPC Alliance (DPCA)**
- Membership organization of DPC physicians (paid membership; the fee is not stated on its public site)
- Newsletter, webinars and an online library (the "DPC Exchange"), largely for members
- [dpcalliance.org](https://www.dpcalliance.org/)

**Direct Primary Care Coalition**
- Advocacy organization focused on federal and state DPC policy
- Policy briefs and legislative updates
- [dpcare.org](https://www.dpcare.org/)

**AAFP DPC Resources**
- [AAFP policy statement on direct primary care](https://www.aafp.org/about/policies/direct-primary-care)
- [AAFP direct primary care page](https://www.aafp.org/practice-operations/practice-and-payment-models/direct-primary-care): 2024 DPC data brief, blog posts, a podcast episode, and a compensation dashboard (member log-in)

**DPC News**
- Daily news and commentary site edited by Douglas Farrago, MD
- Also collects DPC podcast episodes and videos in one place
- Carries advertising from DPC vendors
- [dpcnews.com](https://dpcnews.com/)

### Research

**Academic Articles:**
- Search [PubMed for "direct primary care"](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=%22direct+primary+care%22) for research on outcomes, satisfaction and cost
- The body of peer-reviewed research on DPC is still small; read study design and sample size before quoting a result

## Practice Management

### Healthcare Business

**"The E-Myth Physician" by Michael E. Gerber (2003)**
- Why physician-owned practices struggle
- Systems thinking for medical practices
- Working ON your business, not just IN it
- Widely read by practice owners; predates DPC, so the billing chapters do not apply

**"Start Your Own Medical Practice" by Marlene M. Coleman, MD, and Judge William Huss (Sphinx Publishing, 2006)**
- General startup guide for a fee-for-service practice; not an AAFP publication
- Some sections (entity choice, leases, staffing) still apply to DPC
- Published in 2006 and not revised since; check current availability before buying, as older editions go out of print

**"The Business Side of Medicine: What Medical Schools Don't Teach You" by Tom Harbin, MD (2012)**
- Practice management basics written for physicians
- Financial management
- Operations basics

### Leadership and Management

**"The One Minute Manager" by Ken Blanchard and Spencer Johnson (1982; revised as "The New One Minute Manager", 2015)**
- Quick read on management basics
- Useful when adding staff

**"First, Break All the Rules" by Marcus Buckingham and Curt Coffman (1999)**
- Management and leadership insights
- Based on research
- Helpful for building team culture

## Business Fundamentals

### Entrepreneurship

**"The Lean Startup" by Eric Ries (2011)**
- Build-measure-learn approach
- Minimum viable product concepts
- Testing assumptions before big investments
- Applicable to DPC: Start lean, iterate based on feedback

**"The $100 Startup" by Chris Guillebeau (2012)**
- Starting businesses with minimal capital
- Finding your market
- Lean approach philosophy
- Inspiring for bootstrapped DPC practices

### Marketing

**"Building a StoryBrand" by Donald Miller (2017)**
- Clarifying your message
- Patient (customer) as hero
- Framework for marketing communication
- Applicable to practice messaging; the author also sells a paid framework and coaching built on the book

**"This Is Marketing" by Seth Godin (2018)**
- Modern marketing philosophy
- Finding your smallest viable audience
- Building trust and connection
- Relevant to DPC's relationship-based model

### Pricing and Value

**"Value-Based Fees" by Alan Weiss (2002; later editions 2008 and 2021)**
- Written for consultants, but principles apply
- Pricing based on value, not time
- Mindset for professional services
- Helps think about DPC pricing

## Personal Development

### Productivity and Effectiveness

**"Getting Things Done" by David Allen (2001; revised edition 2015)**
- Personal productivity system
- Managing overwhelm
- Practical methodology
- Helpful for managing solo practice demands

**"Deep Work" by Cal Newport (2016)**
- Focused, valuable work
- Managing distractions
- Relevant for documentation time, complex patients

### Physician Wellbeing

**"Why Doctors Don't Get Rich" by Tom Burns, MD (2020)**
- Physician financial pitfalls
- Building wealth as a physician, with an emphasis on passive-income investing
- Financial independence thinking

**Burnout Resources:**
- Many DPC physicians cite burnout as a reason for leaving fee-for-service practice; DPC changes the workload but does not remove it
- See [Physician Wellbeing](physician-wellbeing.md) for verified support lines and resources
