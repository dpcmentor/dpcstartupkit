# Vermont DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Vermont does not have explicit DPC-enabling legislation. Medication dispensing has restrictions. Small state with rural character. Legal consultation recommended.

---

> [!CAUTION]
> **Verify Current Law:** Vermont lacks explicit DPC legislation. Consult the [Vermont Board of Medical Practice](https://www.healthvermont.gov/systems/board-medical-practice) and a healthcare attorney to ensure compliance.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Status:** No explicit DPC-enabling legislation

Vermont has not enacted specific legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

### What This Means

- Legal status of DPC less certain
- Consult healthcare attorney before starting
- Structure agreements carefully
- Monitor for legislative developments

---

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Vermont has **restrictions** on physician dispensing.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Restrictions:** Limited circumstances for dispensing
- **Pharmacy Board:** [sec.state.vt.us/professional-regulation/list-of-professions/pharmacy.aspx](https://sec.state.vt.us/professional-regulation/list-of-professions/pharmacy.aspx)
- **VPMS:** Check Vermont Prescription Monitoring System
- **Review Regulations:** Consult Board of Pharmacy before implementing

## State-Specific Considerations

### Small Population

- Second least populous state
- Limited patient pool
- Burlington area has most opportunity

### Rural Character

- Very rural state
- Primary care shortage
- Telehealth important

### Health-Conscious Population

- Progressive healthcare attitudes
- Wellness-oriented population
- May value DPC model

### Border Considerations

- Near New Hampshire, Massachusetts, New York
- Cross-state patient considerations

---

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Vermont

- [ ] **Consult healthcare attorney** regarding DPC legal structure
- [ ] Obtain or verify Vermont medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with Secretary of State
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Review dispensing restrictions carefully
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement (attorney review essential)
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
