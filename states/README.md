# State-by-State DPC Guides

State-specific information for Direct Primary Care practices, including DPC legislation, medication dispensing rules, and key regulatory requirements.

## State Categories

### Tier 1: DPC Friendly
States with explicit DPC-enabling legislation and favorable medication dispensing rules.

| State | DPC Law | Dispensing | Guide |
|-------|---------|------------|-------|
| Colorado | Yes (HB 17-1115) | No registration | [Colorado](colorado.md) |
| Florida | Yes (F.S. § 624.27) | Simple registration | [Florida](florida.md) |
| Georgia | Yes (O.C.G.A. § 33-1-19) | Simple notification | [Georgia](georgia.md) |
| Kentucky | Yes (KRS 304.17A-527) | Permitted | [Kentucky](kentucky.md) |
| Maine | Yes | No registration | [Maine](maine.md) |
| Tennessee | Yes (T.C.A. § 56-7-1801) | No registration | [Tennessee](tennessee.md) |
| Wyoming | Yes (Wyo. Stat. § 26-1-104) | No registration | [Wyoming](wyoming.md) |

### Tier 2: Moderate
States with DPC legislation but some restrictions on dispensing or other operational requirements.

| State | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Arizona, Arkansas, Indiana, Kansas, Louisiana, Michigan, Mississippi, Missouri, North Carolina, Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, Texas, Utah, Virginia, Washington, West Virginia | DPC legal; dispensing varies |

### Tier 3: Challenging
States without explicit DPC legislation or with significant restrictions. Legal consultation recommended.

| State | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Massachusetts, New Jersey, New York | Restrictive dispensing; unclear DPC status |
| Others without DPC legislation | Consult healthcare attorney |

## How to Use These Guides

Each state guide includes:
- DPC law status and statutory references
- Medication dispensing regulations
- Licensing and registration requirements
- Key resources and contacts
- State-specific considerations

> [!CAUTION]
> Laws and regulations change. Always verify current requirements with your state medical board, pharmacy board, and a healthcare attorney licensed in your state.

## Federal Updates (2026)

**HSA Eligibility for DPC:** As of January 2026, Health Savings Account funds can be used for DPC membership fees (up to $150/month individual, $300/month family) per H.R. 1/Public Law 119-21.
