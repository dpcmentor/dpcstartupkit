## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Statutes:** Wyo. Stat. Ann. § 26-1-104(a)(vi) and § 26-22-301(c)

**Effective Date:** February 29, 2016

Wyoming was among the first states to enact DPC-enabling legislation, and its statutory language has served as a model for other states.

**Key provisions:**
- DPC agreements explicitly exempted from insurance regulation
- Clear definition of direct primary care arrangements
- Written membership agreement required
- Consumer protection requirements included

### What This Means

- DPC practices operate outside insurance regulatory framework
- Your membership agreements are service contracts, not insurance
- Clear legal standing for the DPC model
- Wyoming's law is often cited as model legislation

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Wyoming allows physician dispensing **without registration requirements**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances (federal requirement)
- **State Permit:** Not required
- **Controlled Substances:** Permitted with DEA registration
- **Labeling:** Follow standard labeling requirements
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

### Practical Implications

Wyoming is among the easiest states for physician dispensing:
- No state paperwork beyond DEA registration
- Can dispense controlled substances with proper DEA compliance
- Focus on patient convenience and cost savings

## Licensing and Registration

### Medical License

**Board:** Wyoming Board of Medicine

**Website:** [wyomedboard.wyo.gov](https://wyomedboard.wyo.gov/)

**Requirements:**
- MD or DO degree from accredited school
- Completion of accredited residency
- Passing examination scores
- Good standing in all jurisdictions

### Business Registration

**Entity Registration:** Wyoming Secretary of State

**Website:** [sos.wyo.gov](https://sos.wyo.gov/)

**Notes:**
- Wyoming is known for business-friendly environment
- No state income tax
- Straightforward LLC formation

### DEA Registration

**Required for:** Prescribing and dispensing controlled substances

**Website:** [deadiversion.usdoj.gov](https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/)

## State-Specific Considerations

### Rural Practice

- Wyoming's low population density creates opportunities in underserved areas
- Telehealth particularly valuable given distances
- House calls may be practical in some areas

### No State Income Tax

Wyoming has no state income tax, which simplifies tax planning and improves take-home income.

### Small Market

- Limited specialist availability in some areas
- Build strong referral relationships
- Consider telehealth partnerships for specialty access

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Wyoming

- [ ] Obtain or verify Wyoming medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with Secretary of State
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement with required disclosures
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
