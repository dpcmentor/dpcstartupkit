# Maryland DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Maryland does not have explicit DPC-enabling legislation. Medication dispensing is permitted with registration. Proximity to DC creates opportunities. Legal consultation recommended.

---

> [!CAUTION]
> **Verify Current Law:** Maryland lacks explicit DPC legislation. Consult the [Maryland Board of Physicians](https://www.mbp.state.md.us/) and a healthcare attorney to ensure compliance.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Status:** No explicit DPC-enabling legislation

Maryland has not enacted specific legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

### What This Means

- Legal status of DPC less certain
- Consult healthcare attorney before starting
- Structure agreements carefully
- Monitor for legislative developments

---

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Maryland permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with Maryland Board of Pharmacy
- **Pharmacy Board:** [health.maryland.gov/pharmacy](https://health.maryland.gov/pharmacy/)
- **PDMP:** Check Maryland Prescription Drug Monitoring Program
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Maryland

- [ ] **Consult healthcare attorney** regarding DPC legal structure
- [ ] Obtain or verify Maryland medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with SDAT
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register with Board of Pharmacy (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement (attorney review essential)
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
