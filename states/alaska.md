# Alaska DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Alaska does not have explicit DPC-enabling legislation, but DPC practices operate under general contract law. Medication dispensing is permitted. Unique geography creates telehealth opportunities.

---

> [!CAUTION]
> **Verify Current Law:** Alaska lacks explicit DPC legislation. Consult the [Alaska State Medical Board](https://www.commerce.alaska.gov/web/cbpl/ProfessionalLicensing/StateMedicalBoard.aspx) and a healthcare attorney to ensure compliance.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Status:** No explicit DPC-enabling legislation

Alaska has not enacted specific legislation exempting DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

### What This Means

- Legal status of DPC less certain
- Consult healthcare attorney before starting
- Structure agreements carefully
- Monitor for legislative developments

---

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Alaska permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with Alaska Board of Pharmacy
- **Pharmacy Board:** [commerce.alaska.gov](https://www.commerce.alaska.gov/web/cbpl/ProfessionalLicensing/BoardofPharmacy.aspx)
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

## State-Specific Considerations

### Unique Geography

- Vast distances between communities
- Many areas accessible only by air
- Telehealth essential

### High Cost of Living

- Among highest cost-of-living states
- Factor into membership pricing
- Supply chain challenges for medications

### No State Income Tax

- Alaska has no state personal income tax
- Also has Permanent Fund Dividend

### Small Population

- Limited patient pool in most areas
- Anchorage has most opportunity
- Consider hybrid models

---

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Alaska

- [ ] **Consult healthcare attorney** regarding DPC legal structure
- [ ] Obtain or verify Alaska medical license
- [ ] Register business entity
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register with Board of Pharmacy (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement (attorney review essential)
- [ ] Establish robust telehealth capabilities
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
