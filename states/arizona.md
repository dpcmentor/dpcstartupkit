# Arizona DPC Guide

> **Quick Summary:** Arizona has DPC-enabling legislation (A.R.S. § 20-123) and permits physician medication dispensing with registration. A business-friendly state with growing DPC presence, particularly in the Phoenix and Tucson metros.

> **Verify Current Law:** Always confirm current regulations with the [Arizona Medical Board](https://www.azmd.gov/) and review [Arizona Revised Statutes Title 20](https://www.azleg.gov/arsDetail/?title=20) for the most current statutory language.

## DPC Law Status

### Legislation

**Statute:** Arizona Revised Statutes § 20-123

Arizona has enacted DPC-enabling legislation that exempts DPC agreements from insurance regulation.

**Key provisions:**
- DPC agreements not considered insurance
- Primary care services defined
- Written agreement required
- Fee limitations for exemption

### What This Means

- DPC practices operate outside insurance regulatory framework
- Legal clarity for membership model
- Standard business regulations apply

---

## Medication Dispensing

### Regulatory Status

Arizona permits physician dispensing **with registration**.

### Requirements

- **DEA Registration:** Required for controlled substances
- **State Registration:** Register with Arizona Board of Pharmacy
- **Controlled Substances:** Subject to Arizona Controlled Substances Act
- **Labeling:** Follow Arizona labeling requirements
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain dispensing records

### Practical Implications

- Registration process required but manageable
- Can dispense most medications with proper compliance
- Review Board of Pharmacy requirements before starting

## State-Specific Considerations

### Growing Markets

- Phoenix metro: Large, growing population
- Tucson: Established medical community
- Suburban and retirement communities offer opportunity

### Retirement Population

- Significant retiree population
- Medicare opt-out implications important
- Clear communication about Medicare status essential

### Business-Friendly Environment

- Arizona known for business-friendly regulations
- Relatively low regulatory burden
- No state tax on Social Security income

---

## Checklist for Starting DPC in Arizona

- [ ] Obtain or verify Arizona medical license
- [ ] Register business entity with Corporation Commission
- [ ] Obtain EIN from IRS
- [ ] Register with DEA (if prescribing controlled substances)
- [ ] Register with Board of Pharmacy (if dispensing)
- [ ] Secure malpractice insurance
- [ ] Develop membership agreement
- [ ] File Medicare opt-out affidavit (if applicable)
