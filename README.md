## What's in this kit

- **Bootstrap path** — start your practice in weeks for under $3,000
- **Business Formation** — legal entities, registration, insurance, partnerships
- **Small Business Fundamentals** — accounting, bookkeeping, cash flow, taxes
- **Pricing & Membership** — pricing strategies, agreements, employer contracts
- **Technology** — EMR selection, telehealth, websites
- **Marketing** — patient education, branding, community outreach
- **Compliance & Legal** — HIPAA, controlled substances, state DPC laws
- **Operations** — office space, equipment, workflows, hiring
- **State Guides** — all 50 states, including DPC legislation and dispensing rules
- **Resources** — organizations, lab partners, recommended reading

## Philosophy

> **Start lean, grow intentionally.**
>
> You don't need expensive systems and elaborate setups to see your first patients. Start with what you have. Add what you need, when revenue justifies it.

Most startup guides assume you have $50,000 and six months to prepare. This kit doesn't. Many successful DPC practices started with a stethoscope, a phone, and patients who needed them — everything else came later, funded by revenue.
