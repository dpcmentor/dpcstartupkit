# Conferences and Events

## Overview

DPC conferences and events provide concentrated learning, networking, and inspiration. Whether you're exploring DPC, just starting, or years into practice, events connect you with the broader DPC community and accelerate your growth.

---

## Major DPC Conferences

### DPC Summit

**Hosted by:** DPC Alliance

**Frequency:** Annual (typically summer)

**Location:** Varies (national locations)

**Audience:** DPC physicians at all stages, staff, industry

**Content:**
- Keynotes from DPC leaders
- Breakout sessions on operations, marketing, clinical topics
- Vendor exhibits
- Networking events
- Pre-conference workshops

**Best for:** Comprehensive DPC education, networking

**Cost:** Typically $300-600 (member discounts available)

**Website:** Check DPC Alliance for current information

---

### Hint Health Summit

**Hosted by:** Hint Health

**Frequency:** Annual

**Audience:** DPC and direct care practices, especially Hint customers

**Content:**
- DPC practice development
- Direct care innovation
- Employer health benefits
- Technology in direct care

**Best for:** Practice growth, employer market, Hint users

**Cost:** Varies

**Website:** Hint Health website

---

### AAFP FMX (Family Medicine Experience)

**Hosted by:** American Academy of Family Physicians

**Frequency:** Annual (typically fall)

**Location:** Varies (major cities)

**Audience:** All family physicians

**DPC content:** Sessions on practice models including DPC

**Best for:** CME plus DPC education, broad family medicine networking

**Cost:** $500-800+ (member pricing)

**Note:** Not DPC-specific, but has DPC tracks and sessions

---

### State and Regional Events

Many states host DPC-focused events:

**Examples:**
- Texas DPC Summit
- California DPC events
- Midwest DPC gatherings
- Various state medical association DPC sessions

**Best for:** Local networking, state-specific issues, lower travel costs

**How to find:** Check state DPC groups, DPC Frontier, state medical associations

---

## Types of Events

### Multi-Day Conferences

**What to expect:**
- Full educational program
- Multiple session tracks
- Vendor exhibits
- Social/networking events
- Optional pre/post workshops

**Investment:** $500-1,500+ (registration + travel)

**Value:** Deep immersion, extensive networking

---

### Single-Day Summits

**What to expect:**
- Focused agenda
- Key topics covered efficiently
- Some networking
- Local or regional

**Investment:** $100-300 (minimal travel if local)

**Value:** Efficient learning, less time away

---

### Webinars and Virtual Events

**What to expect:**
- Online presentations
- Q&A sessions
- On-demand access often available

**Investment:** Free to ~$100

**Value:** No travel, specific topic focus, accessible

**Sources:**
- DPC Alliance
- Hint Health
- DPC Frontier (informal)
- Vendor webinars

---

### Local Meetups

**What to expect:**
- Informal gatherings
- Dinner or lunch meetings
- Peer discussion
- Local focus

**Investment:** Cost of meal

**Value:** Local relationships, practical support

**How to find/start:** DPC Frontier, direct outreach to local DPC docs

## Getting Value from Conferences

### Before the Event

**Preparation:**
- Review speaker list and sessions
- Identify key sessions for your needs
- Research attendees (who do you want to meet?)
- Prepare questions
- Bring business cards
- Plan your schedule

**Mindset:**
- Come ready to learn
- Open to networking
- Specific goals in mind

### During the Event

**Sessions:**
- Attend relevant sessions
- Take notes
- Ask questions
- Engage actively

**Networking:**
- Introduce yourself to others
- Ask about their practice
- Exchange contact information
- Attend social events
- Don't just stick with people you know

**Vendors:**
- Visit exhibit hall
- Learn about new solutions
- Compare options
- Get contact information for follow-up

### After the Event

**Follow-up:**
- Send connection emails within a week
- Connect on LinkedIn
- Review notes
- Implement what you learned
- Schedule follow-up calls with key contacts

**Action:**
- Identify 2-3 things to implement
- Create action items
- Share learning with colleagues/staff
- Plan for next event

### Budget Considerations

**Costs to plan for:**
- Registration fee
- Travel (flight, car)
- Hotel (2-4 nights typically)
- Meals
- Miscellaneous

**Typical total:** $1,000-2,500 per major conference

**Budget-friendly options:**
- Local/regional events
- Virtual events
- Early bird registration
- Membership discounts
- Sharing hotel rooms

## For Those Exploring DPC

### Best First Events

If you're considering DPC:

1. **DPC Summit** - Comprehensive overview
2. **Virtual webinars** - Low commitment introduction
3. **Local DPC meetup** - Personal conversations
4. **DPC tracks at AAFP FMX** - If attending anyway

### What to Learn

- Real physician experiences
- Practical startup guidance
- Challenges and solutions
- Financial realities
- Different models and approaches

### Questions to Ask Practicing DPC Physicians

- What do you wish you knew before starting?
- What were your biggest challenges?
- How long to reach sustainability?
- What would you do differently?
- Any regrets?
